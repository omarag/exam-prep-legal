Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Pro access on a new device?
Open the app, go to the Pro upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account used for your original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Open iPhone Settings > your name (Apple Account) > Subscriptions > Part 107 Drone Coach.

How can I report an issue with a question or explanation?
Email us with the topic and wording of the question so we can review and update it promptly.
