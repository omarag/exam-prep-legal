Privacy Policy — Part 107 Drone Coach

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Part 107 Drone Coach stores your study progress, quiz answers, practice exam scores, and preferences locally on your device. We do not collect your name, physical address, or payment card details.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All payments and subscriptions are processed securely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat solely to validate your Pro entitlement status using an anonymous app user identifier.

3. Study Assistant Features

When you request contextual study help on a question, only the question text and your prompt are transmitted securely to generate an explanation. No personally identifiable information is required.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You can delete all locally stored progress at any time by resetting or uninstalling the app. For privacy inquiries, contact us at: support.app.agu@gmail.com.
